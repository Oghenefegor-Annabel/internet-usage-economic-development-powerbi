# Internet Usage and Economic Development Analysis (2021–2025)

## Overview
This project explores the relationship between internet usage and economic development using the World Bank's World Development Indicators (WDI). The analysis was conducted in Power BI to identify patterns between internet usage, GDP Growth, GDP Per Capita, and Population.

## Problem Statement
As internet access continues to expand globally, it is important to understand whether higher internet usage is associated with stronger economic development. This project explores that relationship across countries from 2021 to 2025.

## Objective
To analyze the relationship between internet usage and economic development using Power BI.

## Dataset
- Source: World Bank World Development Indicators
- Period: 2021–2025
- link : https://datatopics.worldbank.org/world-development-indicators/

## Key Insights
- Eastern and Southern Africa recorded periods of strong GDP growth despite relatively low internet usage.
- Several countries maintained low internet usage throughout the study period.
- Andorra experienced increasing internet usage while GDP growth declined.
- No consistent relationship was observed between internet usage and GDP growth across all countries.
- Burundi recorded low internet usage alongside a low GDP per capita.
- Monaco recorded both high internet usage and one of the highest GDP per capita values.

## Recommendations
- Improve internet infrastructure in countries with low internet penetration.
- Invest in education and innovation alongside digital infrastructure.
- Include more economic indicators in future studies.
